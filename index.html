PocketCoop

Co-op mini-games for Android — play with friends.

PocketCoop is a small collection of local and online co-op mini-games for Android. Sessions, settings and saved games stay in a folder that the app creates in your own Google Drive, so your progress follows you between devices without an account on our side.
