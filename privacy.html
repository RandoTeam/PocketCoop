Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

PocketCoop is an Android application with a collection of co-op mini-games. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is used.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive — files created by the app only. PocketCoop requests the drive.file scope, which gives access only to files and folders that this app itself creates. It cannot see or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • Game data. Session settings, saved games and preferences are written to a single folder created by the app in your Drive.

The app does not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, messages or any other personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

How the information is used

Data in the app folder is used solely to store and synchronise your own game sessions between your own devices. Nothing is sent to servers operated by us — we do not operate any backend for this app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is transmitted only between your device and Google Drive, over connections encrypted with TLS.

Storage and retention

All data remains in your own Google Drive account and is retained until you delete it. Removing the app folder in Drive deletes the stored data. Access tokens are stored only on your device.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer read or write its folder, and any data already stored in your Drive remains under your control.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the project repository.
